Home/Plugin development/Moodle MCP

Your Moodle connected to ChatGPT or Claude.

The Moodle MCP plugin lets ChatGPT or Claude (using the MCP protocol) read and manage authorized Moodle resources while keeping permissions, authentication, and change approvals under Moodle's control.

View plugin on Marketplace ↗← Back to plugin development

Talking about a platform is different from having authorized access to it.

Without an integration, an assistant relies on screenshots, manual exports, and user descriptions. Moodle MCP provides structured tools to inspect real resources and prepare administrative operations, respecting each account's capabilities.

Simplified diagram of the technical flow, not an interface screenshot.

Integration with access controls and human review.

01

OAuth 2.1 and PKCE

Compatible client authorization, with controls for tokens and scopes used by the integration.

02

Separate READ and WRITE operations

Read and write tools are exposed through distinct surfaces and remain subject to actual Moodle permissions.

03

Preview and confirmation

Protected changes are prepared for review and require explicit approval before being saved.

04

Extensible through plugins

Other compatible Moodle components can expose their own tools for reading and operating on resources.

05

Operational integrations

Courses, categories, users, permitted settings, and other resources may be exposed depending on the installation.

06

Permissions and traceability

Making a tool available does not bypass Moodle capabilities or grant permissions beyond those of the connected user.

Real tasks are what make this useful.

READ

“Which courses need attention?”

Inspect connected courses and reports when authorized, without manually exporting data.

ADMINISTRATION

“Prepare this course for creation.”

Prepare a proposed change and present it for approval before saving anything.

CUSTOMIZATION

“Adjust the Moodle home page.”

With LearnLingo installed, read theme data and prepare a design change for confirmation.
