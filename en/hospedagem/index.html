Questions about hosting.

Don't see your situation here? Let's talk about it.

01  Can I start with Moodle already installed?

Yes. Moodle installation is free with every plan, as are migration of an existing platform and creation of a new custom design.

02  What are the plan prices?

Monthly plans are R$ 385 for 50 concurrent students, R$ 650 for 100, R$ 1,300 for 200, and R$ 2,850 for 500. Storage is 40, 60, 100, and 200 GB respectively. Larger operations can request a custom plan.

03  Can I move my existing Moodle?

Yes. Migration is free in every plan. We begin with an assessment of the current platform, then plan transfer and validation of the new environment.

04  Can I use my own domain and plugins?

Yes. You can use your institution's domain, and all plans include a new custom design at no extra charge. Plugins and integrations are reviewed for compatibility and maintenance requirements.
