How to integrate PagSeguro with Moodle.

The setup process must be verified against the installed Kopere Pay version and the current terms of your payment account.

01

Prepare your PagSeguro account

Verify your business account and the credentials required for the available integration.

02

Configure Kopere Pay

Enable PagSeguro, link the courses for sale, and enter integration credentials.

03

Test and certify

Make a test purchase, check the transaction record, and verify correct enrolment after confirmation.

A payment initiated is not necessarily a payment approved.

Credentials must be kept secure, payment amounts must be validated on the server, and enrolment must only be granted after valid provider confirmation.

Questions about Moodle with PagSeguro.

Can I integrate PagSeguro with Moodle?

Yes. Kopere Pay includes a PagSeguro integration for payments linked to Moodle courses.

Are PagSeguro and PagBank related?

Yes. PagSeguro is part of the PagBank ecosystem; products and credentials may differ depending on the contract.

Does the integration support recurring payments?

The code includes utilities for one-off and recurring charges. Your particular scenario should be assessed before deployment.

This integration is part of Kopere Pay.

You do not need a separate product for each payment method. Kopere Pay centralizes sales and enrolment while each gateway has its own credentials and settings.
