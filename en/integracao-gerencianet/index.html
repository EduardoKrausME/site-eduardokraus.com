Gerencianet is now branded Efí. Kopere Pay provides Efí payment methods, connecting course payments with Moodle enrolment.

How to integrate Efí (formerly Gerencianet) with Moodle.

The setup process must be verified against the installed Kopere Pay version and the current terms of your payment account.

01

Configure your Efí account

Create an application in your Efí account and obtain a Client ID and Client Secret.

02

Choose payment methods

Enable the available Kopere Pay methods for bank slip/Pix or cards and configure your courses.

03

Test payment statuses

Verify charges, API callbacks, and enrolment after confirmation before processing real student payments.

A payment initiated is not necessarily a payment approved.

Credentials must be kept secure, payment amounts must be validated on the server, and enrolment must only be granted after valid provider confirmation.
