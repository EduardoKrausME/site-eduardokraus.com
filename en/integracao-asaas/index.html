Moodle integration with Asaas.

When selling courses through Asaas, Kopere Pay links payments to registration and enrolment in Moodle so your institution doesn't have to process every enrolment manually.

How to integrate Asaas with Moodle.

The setup process must be verified against the installed Kopere Pay version and the current terms of your payment account.

01

Get your Asaas API key

Use an active Asaas account, generate an API key, and keep it secure on the server.

02

Enable the payment method in Kopere Pay

Configure the method, instalments where applicable, and course prices.

03

Verify the flow

Test a charge, check its status, and confirm enrolment only after valid payment confirmation.

A payment initiated is not necessarily a payment approved.

Credentials must be kept secure, payment amounts must be validated on the server, and enrolment must only be granted after valid provider confirmation.

Questions about Moodle with Asaas.

Can I integrate Asaas with Moodle?

Yes. Kopere Pay supports Asaas payment methods for Moodle courses.

Can students pay by bank slip or card?

Separate implementations exist for bank slips and cards. Availability depends on account settings and the installed version.

Does the student get access before paying?

Automated enrolment must depend on valid payment confirmation, not simply the creation of a charge.

This integration is part of Kopere Pay.

You do not need a separate product for each payment method. Kopere Pay centralizes sales and enrolment while each gateway has its own credentials and settings.
