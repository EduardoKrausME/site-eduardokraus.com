Manage sales and enrolment in one flow.

When finance and administration operate on different data, billing and access become support tasks. The system brings together the records needed to investigate a transaction and track its status.

The amount charged must never depend on the student's browser.

Payment integrations must validate amounts and rules on the server, process gateway confirmations, and never trust prices supplied by the client. Kopere Pay was designed around this payment and enrolment flow.

Transaction monitoring helps distinguish an initial payment request from confirmed payment, preventing unauthorized access.

Before integrating payments with Moodle.

Does Kopere Pay automatically enrol students?

The flow is designed to receive payment confirmation and enrol students according to course rules and settings.

Do I need to create all my courses again?

The system integrates with existing Moodle courses, avoiding a separate catalog for each course. Prices and billing are configured in the plugin.

Which Moodle versions are supported?

The original page mentions support starting with Moodle 4.5. Before installing, verify compatibility between the latest plugin version, your Moodle version, and the payment integrations you use.

Can I use Kopere Pay on a Moodle hosted by you?

Yes. We can assess installation and configuration based on your Moodle version and required integrations. Request a quote to define the requirements.
