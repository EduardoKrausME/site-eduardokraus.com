MOODLE MOBILE FOR INSTITUTIONS

Your Moodle, your app, your brand.

Students sign in with their existing Moodle credentials and get a mobile-friendly experience, while your institution retains its visual identity. Integration uses Moodle services and respects the permissions and features available on the platform.

01

Visual identity

Customize the name, icon, colors, splash screen, and login appearance to match your institution's branding.

02

Courses, grades, and profile

Access courses, content, grades, and profile data, authenticated through Moodle's mobile services.

03

Offline content

Download compatible files, resources, and activities for offline study when Moodle and the content support it.

04

Educational features

Mobile support for pages, files, folders, links, SCORM, H5P, SuperVideo, and other supported activities.

05

Push notifications

Notifications can take students directly to Moodle links and activities, depending on configuration.

06

Publication and maintenance

Build Android and iOS versions, publish to the stores, and monitor updates and compatibility.

QG do ENEM on your phone.

In 2018, QG do ENEM officially introduced its Android and iOS app, bringing together classes, study resources, and progress-tracking features in a mobile-friendly experience.

Students could choose a course, download video lessons to watch offline, access free classes, follow news, and check events and live broadcasts. The original announcement documents these features and shows how mobile was already part of educational operations.
