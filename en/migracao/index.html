Every step has a purpose.

A migration should not be a black box. The work is organized so you understand what will be done and what needs to be checked.

01

Understand what is already there.

Moodle version, theme, plugins, database, files, integrations, and institutional requirements.

02

Prepare the new infrastructure.

Capacity planning, transfer and rollback strategy, maintenance window, and user communications.

03

Transfer and verify.

Check courses, users, files, access, settings, activities, scheduled tasks, and agreed integrations.

04

Support the go-live transition.

Coordinate the domain switch, verify operation after migration, and advise on next steps.

What to agree on before migrating.

Don't see your situation here? Let's talk about it.

01  Is there an additional migration fee?

No. All hosting plans include free migration, along with Moodle installation and a new custom design at no extra charge.

02  Will Moodle be unavailable?

A maintenance window may be needed for the final transfer and domain change. It is scheduled around your platform and your institution's routine.

03  Does migration also include an upgrade?

These are separate tasks that may be part of the same project. Upgrades require reviewing the current Moodle version, plugins, theme, and integrations.

04  How is data verified?

The validation plan covers transferred data and the workflows included in the agreed scope, such as access, courses, activities, and integrations.

05  Can I keep the same address?

Yes. Your domain can be preserved, with DNS changes coordinated after the new platform has been prepared and validated.
