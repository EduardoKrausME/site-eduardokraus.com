A learning platform is used by people with different goals and routines. For teachers, technology should help organize their work; for students, it should let learning happen.

That's where I focus my work. I develop Moodle plugins, customizations, and infrastructure, considering both how the platform runs and how technical decisions affect long-term maintenance.

In hosting, this means understanding operations before sizing the environment, checking compatibility before changes, and clearly defining ongoing support.
